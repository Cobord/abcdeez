Adaptive Learning System User Manual

Getting Started

Welcome to the Adaptive Learning System! This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to begin your learning journey. In just a few minutes, you'll be up and running with your first training session.

Quick Start (3 Minutes)

If you want to dive right in:

  1. Launch the application on your device
  2. Click "Start as Guest" on the welcome screen
  3. Select "Quick Tour 📚" for a guided introduction
  4. Choose a learning domain (we recommend starting with Alphabet)
  5. Click "Start Training Session" to begin learning!

That's it! You're now experiencing adaptive learning tailored to your performance.

Overview of the User Interface

When you first open the application, you'll see five main areas:

🏠 Welcome Screen

Your starting point, featuring:

  • Login options for returning users
  • Guest mode for quick access
  • Demo showcase buttons
  • Navigation to all major sections

📚 Domain Selection

Where you choose what to learn:

  • Alphabet: Letter sequences (A→B→C)
  • Days of Week: Temporal patterns (Mon→Tue→Wed)
  • Music: Musical scales (C→D→E)
  • Mathematics: Number operations (1+1=2)

🎯 Training Area

Your main learning environment:

  • Task display with clear prompts
  • Multiple choice answer buttons
  • Hint system for assistance
  • Real-time feedback
  • Progress tracking

📊 Performance Dashboard

Your progress hub showing:

  • Overall accuracy and speed metrics
  • Learning curves and trends
  • Cognitive performance indicators
  • Session history

⚙️ Settings

Customize your experience:

  • Learning preferences
  • Display options
  • Demo settings
  • Data management

Your First Session: Step by Step

Step 1: Choose Your Path

You have three options to get started:

Option A: Guest Mode (Fastest)

  • Click "Start as Guest"
  • Perfect for trying out the system
  • No registration required
  • Data saved locally only

Option B: Create Account (Recommended)

  • Click "Sign Up"
  • Enter username, email, and password
  • Enables cloud sync and progress tracking
  • Access from multiple devices

Option C: Guided Demo (Best for First-Timers)

  • Click "Quick Tour 📚"
  • Interactive walkthrough of all features
  • Learn by watching and doing
  • Takes about 5 minutes

Step 2: Select a Learning Domain

For your first session, we recommend:

  • Beginners: Start with Alphabet - it's familiar and intuitive
  • Music lovers: Try Musical Notes - learn scales while training
  • Math enthusiasts: Choose Mathematics - practice mental arithmetic
  • Looking for a challenge: Select Days of Week - master cyclic patterns

Each domain targets different cognitive skills:

  • Sequential memory (Alphabet)
  • Cyclic patterns (Days)
  • Interval relationships (Music)
  • Numerical operations (Math)

Step 3: Configure Your Session

Before starting, you can adjust:

Adaptive Scheduling

  • ✅ Enabled (Recommended): AI selects optimal tasks
  • ❌ Disabled: Random task selection

Hints System

  • ✅ Enabled (Recommended): Get help when struggling
  • ❌ Disabled: Challenge yourself without assistance

Difficulty Level

  • Easy (0-30%): Great for warming up
  • Medium (40-60%): Optimal learning zone
  • Hard (70-100%): Push your limits

Step 4: Start Training!

Click "Start Training Session" and you'll see your first task:

  1. Read the prompt carefully (e.g., "What comes after B?")
  2. Consider your options (A, B, C, D buttons)
  3. Select your answer by clicking a button
  4. Receive instant feedback (Correct! or Try again)
  5. Continue to next task or request a hint if stuck

Performance Metrics

Your session progress shows:

  • Tasks: Number completed
  • Accuracy: Percentage correct
  • Streak: Current correct answers in a row
  • Time: Session duration

Feedback System

After each answer:

  • ✅ Correct: Positive reinforcement + next task
  • ❌ Incorrect: Shows correct answer + learning opportunity
  • 💡 Hint Used: Tracked for adaptive difficulty

Navigation Tips

Keyboard Shortcuts (Desktop)

  • Enter - Submit answer / Continue
  • 1-4 or A-D - Select answer option
  • H - Request hint
  • P - Pause session
  • Esc - Open menu

Touch Gestures (Mobile)

  • Tap - Select answer
  • Swipe left/right - Navigate screens
  • Long press - View detailed information
  • Pinch - Zoom charts (Dashboard)

Making the Most of Your First Session

Do's ✅

  • Take your time - There's no time pressure
  • Use hints when genuinely stuck
  • Review feedback carefully
  • Complete 10-20 tasks for meaningful metrics
  • Check your dashboard after the session

Don'ts ❌

  • Don't rush - Speed comes with practice
  • Don't guess randomly - Think before answering
  • Don't skip feedback - It's valuable learning
  • Don't worry about mistakes - They're part of learning

What Happens Behind the Scenes?

While you're answering questions, the system is:

  1. Tracking your responses - Building your knowledge model
  2. Calculating metrics - Analyzing your learning patterns
  3. Adapting difficulty - Finding your optimal challenge level
  4. Selecting next tasks - Choosing what you need to practice
  5. Generating hints - Preparing contextual assistance

After Your First Session

Once you complete your first training session:

  1. Visit the Dashboard to see your performance metrics
  2. Review your learning curve to understand progress
  3. Check cognitive metrics for deeper insights
  4. Export your data if you want to analyze it further
  5. Plan your next session based on the feedback

Common First-Time Questions

Q: How many tasks should I complete? A: Aim for 15-20 tasks in your first session for meaningful data.

Q: Should I use hints? A: Yes! Hints are learning tools, not cheats. Use them when stuck.

Q: What if I make many mistakes? A: Perfect! The system learns from your errors to help you improve.

Q: Can I pause and resume? A: Yes, click "Pause" anytime. Your progress is saved.

Q: How often should I practice? A: Daily 10-minute sessions are better than weekly hour-long ones.
