# handlers/audit_retention.rs - Audit Data Retention Management

## Requirements and Dataflow
- Manages audit log retention policies with automated cleanup and compliance
- Provides retention statistics and compliance reporting for regulatory requirements
- Implements configurable retention policies with legal and business requirements
- Supports audit trail management with secure deletion and archive capabilities
- Handles compliance reporting with regulatory audit and certification support

## Key Abstractions and Interfaces
- Retention policy configuration with automated enforcement and compliance tracking
- Cleanup operations with secure data deletion and privacy protection
- Compliance reporting with regulatory requirement adherence and audit trail documentation
- Archive management with long-term storage and retrieval capabilities

## Dependencies and Interactions
- **Audit System**: Integration with comprehensive audit logging and event tracking
- **Compliance Framework**: Regulatory requirement adherence and certification support
- **Data Privacy**: Secure deletion and privacy protection for retained data
- **Archive Systems**: Long-term storage management with retrieval and compliance support
