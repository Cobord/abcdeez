# handlers/research.rs - Research Data Management

## Requirements and Dataflow
- Manages research data collection with proper anonymization and consent tracking
- Provides research participant management with ethics compliance
- Implements data export functionality for academic research and publication
- Supports research study coordination with protocol adherence
- Handles research data privacy with institutional review board (IRB) compliance

## Key Abstractions and Interfaces
- Research participant enrollment with consent management and tracking
- Anonymized data collection with privacy protection and ethics compliance
- Research data export with institutional approval and participant consent verification
- Study protocol management with research methodology compliance

## Dependencies and Interactions
- **Ethics Framework**: IRB compliance and research participant protection
- **Data Privacy**: Research data anonymization and secure handling
- **Participant Management**: Consent tracking and enrollment coordination
- **Export Control**: Academic data sharing with proper approvals and protections
