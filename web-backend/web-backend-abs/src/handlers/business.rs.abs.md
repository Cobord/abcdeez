# handlers/business.rs - Business Intelligence and Metrics

## Requirements and Dataflow
- Provides comprehensive business dashboard with key performance indicators
- Generates revenue metrics and user journey analytics for business intelligence
- Implements learning effectiveness measurement for product optimization
- Supports business data export for analysis and reporting
- Tracks daily metrics and operational performance indicators

## Key Abstractions and Interfaces
- Business dashboard with executive-level KPIs and operational metrics
- Revenue analytics with subscription tracking and financial performance
- Learning effectiveness measurement with educational outcome analysis
- User journey analytics with engagement and retention tracking
- Daily metrics aggregation with trend analysis and reporting

## Dependencies and Interactions
- **User Analytics**: User behavior tracking and engagement measurement
- **Revenue Systems**: Subscription management and financial tracking
- **Learning Analytics**: Educational effectiveness and outcome measurement
- **Business Intelligence**: Executive reporting and strategic metrics
